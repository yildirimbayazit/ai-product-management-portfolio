Product Management Approach

My approach to AI product management is centered around five principles:

1. Start with the problem — Understand the business and customer problem before selecting a technology.
2. Define measurable value — Connect product decisions to clear business and user outcomes.
3. Use AI where it creates leverage — AI should solve a meaningful problem rather than being added simply because it is available.
4. Bridge business and technology — Effective AI products require alignment between business strategy, product requirements, and technical execution.
5. Iterate and learn — Validate assumptions, measure results, and continuously improve the product.
